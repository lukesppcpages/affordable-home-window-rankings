## Reputation (themes, not scores)
Themes summarized from public reviews, checked October 2026. No rating or review count is
shown on the page because those numbers change and the brief rules out scores.
- Trustpilot — https://www.trustpilot.com/review/powerhrg.com (large volume; praise for crews,
  communication; complaints about sales pressure, price, quality issues)
- Checkbook — https://www.checkbook.org/md/greenbelt/roofers/power-home-remodeling/qpky4
  (price higher than other local contractors; slow service response)
- WDIV ClickOnDetroit, 2018 — https://clickondetroit.com/consumer/2018/05/03/metro-detroiters-report-aggressive-pushy-salespeople-from-southfield-business
  (pushy sales complaints; Power said it would review sales training)
